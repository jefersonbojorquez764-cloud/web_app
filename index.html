Mount Fuji

The highest mountain in Japan. It stands at 3,776 meters (12,388 feet) tall. Located on Honshu island just west of Tokyo, this famous stratovolcano has a nearly symmetrical snow-capped cone that serves as an enduring cultural and spiritual symbol of Japan.
